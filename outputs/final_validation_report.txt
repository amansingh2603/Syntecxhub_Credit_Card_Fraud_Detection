Fraud detection is an imbalanced classification problem where missing fraudulent transactions can be costly.
A lower decision threshold generally increases recall and detects more potential fraud cases, but may also increase false positives.
A higher threshold generally increases precision, but may allow more fraudulent transactions to remain undetected.
The selected threshold is an analytical starting point based on F1 score and should be adjusted using real business costs before production deployment.
